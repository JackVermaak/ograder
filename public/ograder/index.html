    What is an O-grade?

    An O-grade rates a rock climb by how hard it is for Adam Ondra. The Wide Boyz invented the scale and Ondra extended it: O0 is a climb he flashes, O1 takes him one session, O2 takes two, and Silence, the world's first 9c, is O60. Warm-ups get minus grades.

    The O-Grader covers every sport route and boulder in his logbook. For a climb he hasn't done, estimate its O-grade from its grade, or read how the O-grade scale works.
